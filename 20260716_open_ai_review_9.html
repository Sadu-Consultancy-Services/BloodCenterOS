Design decisions:

  • Direct INSERTs instead of SP calls — simpler, faster, no dependency on SP logic for seed data
  • Explicit IDs with ON CONFLICT (PK) DO NOTHING — idempotent, multi-run safe
  • Sequence advancement via setval() after seeding — prevents PK conflicts when the app inserts new rows
  • CenterId = NULL for lookup values — system-wide defaults, not center-specific
  • Service charges at Center 1 — matches the single seed center from seed_test.sql
